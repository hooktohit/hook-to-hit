Before you buy

What this course will and won't do

It will teach you to build, publish and monetize a casual mobile game, using ads that show your real gameplay.

It will not promise income. Most first games earn little or nothing; you will likely build several small games before one tests well.

It will not teach fake-gameplay ads or real-money games. Fake ads get banned, and money games are illegal in India.

You need a laptop or desktop that can run Unity, and an Android phone for testing. A phone alone is not enough.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need to know coding?

No. Modules 1 to 4 need no code. Module 5 starts from C# basics with free Unity tutorials and two short starter scripts.

Is the course in Hindi?

The lessons are in simple English. Module 6 shows how to add Hindi and regional languages to your own game.

How much money do I need besides the course?

Google Play's one-time $25 developer fee (roughly ₹2,400). Unity and the other tools are free for beginners. Ad testing is optional and can start small.

Can someone under 18 publish a game?

Google requires developer account holders to be 18 or older. Younger learners can study and build, and publish later or with a parent's guidance on what is allowed.

Is this legal in India?

Yes. Casual and social games are allowed under the Online Gaming Act 2025. The course does not cover real-money games, which are banned.

How long do I get access?

Your access code keeps working on this site. Lessons are updated when Google's rules change.
